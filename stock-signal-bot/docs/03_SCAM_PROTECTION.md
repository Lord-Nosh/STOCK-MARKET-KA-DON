# Scam Protection & Reality Checks

1. **Win Rate vs Risk-to-Reward (R:R)**:
   - High-end quant desks average **55% to 65% win rates**.
   - A strategy claiming 90%+ is either overfitted, repainting, or fraudulent.
   - At 1:2 R:R, a 50% win rate produces solid net profit.

2. **Repainting Prevention**:
   - Never trigger a signal on an open, incomplete candle. Signals evaluate strictly on **closed candle bars**.

3. **Friction, Taxes & Slippage**:
   - Incurred costs on Indian intraday: STT, GST (18%), Stamp Duty, SEBI charges, Brokerage.
   - Micro-scalps (< 0.4%) lose money after friction. Minimum target move must exceed **0.7%**.

4. **Circuit Limit Protections**:
   - Only trade **F&O constituent stocks** (Nifty 100). Non-F&O stocks can lock into 5% lower circuits, preventing exit and triggering auction penalties.
