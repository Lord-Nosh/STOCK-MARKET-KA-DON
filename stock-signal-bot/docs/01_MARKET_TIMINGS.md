# Indian Market Timing Windows (IST)

| Window (IST) | Market Phase | Action |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **09:00 - 09:08** | Pre-Market Auction | Track gap-up / gap-down discovery. No trading. |
| **09:15 - 09:30** | Opening Trap Zone | **HARD NO ENTRY**. High spread, SL hunting, institutional re-balancing. |
| **09:30 - 11:15** | **Prime Momentum #1** | **Active entries**. High volume, ORB, and VWAP crossovers. |
| **11:15 - 13:15** | Midday Consolidation | Volume drops. Avoid fresh breakouts. Trail existing stop-losses. |
| **13:15 - 14:45** | **Prime Momentum #2** | **Directional continuation**. European overlap trend synchronization. |
| **14:45 - 15:15** | Square-off Window | Mandatory profit lock. Groww MIS auto-squares off ~15:15. |
| **15:15 - 15:30** | Closing Auction | Delivery (CNC) marks only. |
